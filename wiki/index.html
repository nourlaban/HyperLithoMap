◆ Key Technical Concepts

🌈

Spectral Fingerprinting

Each mineral has unique absorption bands across 400–2500 nm. 234 bands capture diagnostic features invisible to RGB or multispectral sensors.

📐

Spatial–Spectral Fusion

Deep models extract 5×5 pixel patches and process spatial context together with spectral content, resolving mixed pixels at geological boundaries.

⚖️

Nodata-Aware Normalisation

Nodata pixels (−9999) are excluded from per-band min–max normalisation, preserving full spectral contrast between geological units.

🎯

Class-Weighted Loss

Inverse-frequency class weights prevent model collapse on rare but scientifically important classes such as alteration zones and serpentinites.
